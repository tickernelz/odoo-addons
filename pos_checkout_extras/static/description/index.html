POS Checkout Extras

A known customer, a payment reference, and the right analytic distribution on every POS sale.

Customer required

  • Not required: no change.
  • Required at order: Pay asks for a customer first.
  • Required at payment: Validate asks for a customer first.

The Customer button shows a red Required badge until a customer is set. Refunds and unpaid draft orders are never blocked. The server refuses a paid order without a customer when it was dated after the option was turned on.

Payment reference

Turn on Payment Reference for a point of sale and the payment screen gets an Add Payment Reference button.

The cashier types a transfer number, an EDC approval code or a voucher number (up to 64 characters). It is printed on the receipt and kept on the order and on each of its payments, where it can be searched, filtered and grouped.

Analytic per counter

Give each point of sale one analytic distribution, across several plans if needed.

When a session closes it is written on the journal items: sales, cost of goods sold, cash and bank closing differences, and the invoices of its orders. Choose Profit and loss lines (default) or All lines.

How to use

  1. Point of Sale, Configuration, Settings: pick the point of sale at the top.
  2. In Checkout Extras, choose when a customer is required and whether a payment reference can be added.
  3. Set the Analytic Distribution of the counter (requires Analytic Accounting in the Accounting settings).
  4. For sessions closed before, click Synchronise this counter or Synchronise all counters, check the preview, then Synchronise.

Good to know

  • Changing a distribution only affects sessions closed afterwards. Existing entries change only through Synchronise, and every change is recorded in the Analytic Sync Log.
  • Synchronise updates up to 10,000 journal items per click and shows what remains.
  • Reporting, POS Analytic Coverage prints which counters and journal items carry their distribution.
  • Orders: filters Missing customer and With payment reference, and a Payment Reference column. Payments: the same reference, with pivot and graph views. Reporting, Orders: the reference is a field of the POS analysis.
  • Odoo does not enforce a mandatory analytic plan on the journal entries of a POS session. A counter without a distribution closes normally; the coverage report shows its items as missing.
  • Uninstalling keeps the distributions already written on journal items.
