POS Promotions

Tiered promotions, exclusive groups and happy hours on top of Discount & Loyalty

  • Tiered promotions: spending thresholds with a percentage or an amount per order; the highest tier reached applies, with an optional cap and rounding once per order.
  • Exclusive groups on promotions and Discount & Loyalty programmes: only the largest discount of a group applies; on equal amounts the lower priority number wins.
  • Happy hours on promotions and programmes, in the timezone of each point of sale; the cashier is told when a window ends during an order.
  • Cashier screen: an upsell banner on the till and the customer display, a happy hour badge with the time left, and the promotion name on the order line and receipt.
  • Approval: draft, waiting approval, approved, active, ended, stopped; only drafts can change.
  • Discounts are ordinary order lines with the promotion's discount product, so journals, taxes and reports stay native. Refunds return their share automatically, and promotions work offline.
